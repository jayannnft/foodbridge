# 🌱 FoodBridge

**A responsive web platform that connects local restaurants, bakeries, and grocery stores with nearby charities and community organizations to donate unsold surplus food and reduce food waste.**

---

## 📖 Overview

Every day, local food businesses are left with wholesome, unsold inventory at closing time, while neighborhood shelters and community kitchens need fresh supplies. **FoodBridge** streamlines local food rescue by giving food providers a fast way to list surplus items and allowing verified community organizations to browse, claim, and coordinate pickups in real time.

---

## ✨ Core Features

### For Food Providers (Restaurants, Bakeries & Grocers)
* **Quick Surplus Posting:** List available food in seconds with quantity estimates, food categories, best-before timestamps, and pickup time windows.
* **Clear Allergen & Ingredient Disclosures:** Provide vital dietary and handling notes so recipient organizations know exactly what they are collecting.
* **Pickup Management (`My Listings`):** Track which charity has claimed a donation and mark items as **Collected** once handed off.

### For Recipients (Charities, Shelters & Community Kitchens)
* **Live Surplus Feed:** Browse available donations across your city in a clean, scannable card layout.
* **Smart Search & Filtering:** Filter listings by **Food Category** (Prepared Meals, Bakery, Fresh Produce, Dairy, Pantry), **Pickup Area**, and **Availability Status**.
* **Instant Claiming & Duplicate Protection:** Claim available food with one click. Once claimed, the listing locks automatically to prevent double-booking by other organizations.
